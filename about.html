Our story

Built for the world,
from day one.

Aurora was started with a simple conviction: ambitious founders deserve a product partner that thinks as big as they do. We set out to be that partner.

19
2019

Aurora Systems founded

Incorporated as a software studio with a clear mission: build world-class digital products for founders who refuse to settle for average.

21
2021

First fintech product

Shipped Bleu, our first neobank product. Proved that world-class fintech UX could be designed and built by a lean, ambitious team.

23
2023

US expansion & AI pivot

Opened US operations in Delaware. Reorganised as Attribute AI, LLC with Aurora as the product innovation brand. Went AI-native across all services.

30+ products. Global clients.

Operating across two continents, shipping products for founders and enterprises from Harare to Silicon Valley. The mission hasn't changed — only the scale.
