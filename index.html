FAQ

Things we get asked.

How fast can we get started?
+
Most engagements kick off within a week. We don't have six-week onboarding cycles. Once we're aligned on scope, we move. Discovery workshops can start as soon as 3–5 days from first contact.
Do we have to sign a long contract?
+
No. We start with a scoped engagement — usually a validation sprint or strategy phase. Long-term retainers are earned through results, not locked in upfront. You stay because the value is clear.
We already have developers. Can you still help?
+
Absolutely. We can embed alongside your team for specific capabilities — AI integration, product strategy, design, or to accelerate delivery. We're not territorial; we're outcome-driven.
What do we actually get at the end?
+
A working product in your hands — with all IP, source code, documentation, and deployment infrastructure fully owned by you. No lock-in, no black boxes.
Do you work with early-stage startups?
+
Yes — founders are our favourite clients. We've helped teams go from napkin sketch to funded product. If you have a problem worth solving and the commitment to see it through, we want to talk.
